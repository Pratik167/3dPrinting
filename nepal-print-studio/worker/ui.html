A few useful answers

Before we hit print.

Do I need a ready-to-print 3D model?

No. Send a description, dimensions, sketch or reference photo. Design work and revisions will be scoped and quoted separately.

How much will my print cost?

It depends on design work, dimensions, material, print duration, quantity and finishing. Catalogue prices are planning estimates. Your final quotation confirms the total and delivery charges.

Can you print any model I upload?

Every model requires review for geometry, wall thickness, scale and material suitability. We focus on decorative items, prototypes and non-safety-critical parts.

When will my order be ready?

A timeframe is agreed with your quotation based on design approval, print complexity and the production queue. Please mention any deadline in your request.

Can I pay online?

Payment is arranged after you approve a quotation. Merchant checkout is not active yet. Payment instructions will be confirmed directly before production.
